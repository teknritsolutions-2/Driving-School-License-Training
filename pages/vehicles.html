Choosing Your Training Vehicle

Match the Vehicle to Your Learning Goal

The best training environment depends on the licence category, transmission and road skills you want to practise.

Automatic Confidence

Sedans provide a familiar driving position for foundational control, parking and everyday traffic routines.

Higher-View Practice

The compact SUV helps learners understand a taller seating position, wider vehicle awareness and blind-spot checks.

Specialist Controls

Manual and motorcycle options support learners who need clutch, balance or category-specific control practice.

Before the Lesson

A Consistent Vehicle Setup Comes First

Every practical session begins with a learner-focused setup so the controls, mirrors and driving position are understood before moving away.

  • Seat, steering and mirrors adjusted for comfortable control and clear observation.
  • Primary controls and dashboard warnings reviewed before the engine is started.
  • Instructor controls and the communication plan explained before entering traffic.
  • Vehicle choice revisited if the learner's course or confidence level changes.
