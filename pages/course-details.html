Beginner Driver License Program

Comprehensive 20-hour driver education track covering essential controls, precision maneuvers, and road test readiness.

20 Total In-Car Hours Automatic Dual-Control Sedan 1-on-1 Certified Instructor Certificate of Completion

Course Overview & Learning Objectives

Our Beginner Driver License Program is engineered to take learners with zero previous driving experience to complete, independent road test readiness. The course is divided into four sequential 5-hour learning blocks with real-time instructor evaluations logged in your student portal.

Every lesson is conducted in a dual-control vehicle with a dedicated primary instructor. We provide complimentary home pickup, online study materials, and vehicle rental on the day of your official road test.

Curriculum Syllabus & Milestones

  • Pre-drive safety walkaround & cockpit adjustment
  • Three-mirror alignment & blind spot awareness
  • Hand-over-hand steering and progressive braking
  • Quiet residential neighborhood driving & stop line rules

  • Parallel parking between cones using the 45-degree method
  • 90-degree forward and reverse bay parking
  • 3-point turnaround (K-turns) on narrow roads
  • 4-way stop intersection right-of-way priority

  • Multi-lane roundabout entry, circulating, and exit signaling
  • Highway on-ramp acceleration & safe merging gap judgment
  • Safe following distances at 55+ MPH speeds
  • Night driving glare adaptation & high-beam rules

  • Exact DMV test center route simulation drives
  • Scored with state critical-fault evaluation rubric
  • Pre-exam mechanical safety demonstration check
  • Final warm-up drive and test day vehicle escort
